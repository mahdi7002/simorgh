## Why these venues, not every event listing

These are selected because their official calls match one of three real outputs: a policy-oriented research article, a reproducible Persian NLP evaluation, or an actual poster/demo of digital cultural heritage. Search-engine calendars and conference aggregators are discovery tools, not enough proof of peer review, publication, indexing or eligibility. Do not pay a fee or claim Scopus/Web of Science indexing based only on an event listing.

## Submission-integrity rules

1. The national article remains the first deadline-driven deliverable. The DH poster/demo can be a separate description of a real interactive work, not a disguised third research paper.
2. Before sending overlapping experimental work to SilkRoadNLP or AbjadNLP, compare their double-submission/archival policies with the national conference's policy. Do not submit the same manuscript to both workshops.
3. No acceptance, indexing, prize, travel funding or eligibility is guaranteed by this tracker.
4. Links are retained so each date can be reopened and rechecked. Review deadlines again before submission.
5. The current repository's provenance audit remains a blocker for claims about clean-clone reproducibility and redistribution of the poetry/Quran data.
